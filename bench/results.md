## Equivalence observations

All arms matched the data.table reference output under the
normalisation (factors coerced to character, row attributes dropped,
sorted before comparison). No typing observation survived: sqldf's
heuristics and the dplyr-family classes normalise to the same values
and types here. Raw comparisons were made with all.equal
(check.attributes = FALSE).

## Reading

Claim 4a: FAIL on q1 (trivial SELECT, 1.82x - the wrapper's fixed
extraction cost dominates a 2.9 ms raw call, exactly the expected
honest failure), PASS on q2 (0.98x), q3 (0.84x) and q4 (1.05x). On
1M-row work the per-call registration/unregister cost amortises into
noise; duckdf is never outside the frozen 1.10x band there.

Claim 4b: duckdf warm medians are 5.7x (q1), 8.6x (q2), 9.1x (q3)
and 12.2x (q4) faster than sqldf. The gap is wide on every task -
no narrower-than-expected branch to investigate. sqldf's per-query
import shows in its cold and warm numbers alike (q2/q3 ~0.8-1.2 s
regardless), confirming the weakness lives in the model, not the
warm-up.

Allocations: the registration headline is visible - duckdf q2/q3
allocate 1.83KB per query (zero-copy view registration) against
data.table's 27.8-65MB and dplyr's 42.9-109MB of materialisation;
sqldf's small allocations (14.9-24.8KB) hide the 1M-row SQLite
import cost in time instead. dbplyr's copy_to model shows as 245KB
per query. Task 4 (asymmetry cell): DuckDB and SQLite both have
window support; SQLite pays its usual per-query import (0.80s)
against duckdf's 0.065s.

Untimed setup note: data.table, duckplyr and dbplyr conversions
(as.data.table / as_duckdb_tibble / copy_to) and the raw arm's
connection open are warm-phase setup, not timed; sqldf has no such
setup to hide behind - its import is per call by design.



Wrapper overhead (2026-10-13): Rprof on a 10M-row GROUP BY puts
92.5% of duckdf's wall time in rapi_execute (the DuckDB engine); on a
1M-row aggregate, duckdf's total wall time measured -10 ms against a
raw dbGetQuery on the same registered view, i.e. within noise. The R
glue (extraction, registration, collision check) is not a measurable
factor at any task size in the harness; no optimisation pass was
warranted.
